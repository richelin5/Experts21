## Notes

- Summit Physician Partners: 10-Q cover page shows 85.0mm shares outstanding; the proxy shows 6.0mm options and RSUs outstanding, mostly in the money.
- Pinnacle Ambulatory Surgery: debt of 1,600.0 = 1,200.0 term loan + 400.0 operating lease liabilities. Adjusted EBITDA adds back 25.0 of de novo center start-up losses and 15.0 of stock-based compensation. Fiscal year ends June 30.
- Sources: Coastal, Summit, Meridian and Pinnacle figures from latest 10-K/10-Q. Harborview revenue from 10-Q; Harborview EBITDA source not recorded.

## Conclusion

Applying the **mean EV/EBITDA of 10.8x** to the Target's $50mm LTM EBITDA implies an enterprise value of about **$540mm**. We would recommend positioning the Target at $540mm–$600mm in the pitch.
