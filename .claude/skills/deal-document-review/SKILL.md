# Deal document review

Reference: `docs/01-middle-market-ma-advisory.md` section 7.

## Page-turn checklist

1. **Storyline:** can the "why this asset, why now, why this buyer" story be stated in three sentences? Does every page earn its place?
2. **Investment highlights backed by evidence:** each claim ("market leader", "de novo engine", "payer diversification") is tied to a number, a source and a page.
3. **Numbers tie:** the same revenue, EBITDA, location count and provider count appear identically on every page and match the model; footnotes define adjusted EBITDA.
4. **Healthcare KPIs present:** visits, RVUs, census or ADC, length of stay, revenue per visit, payer mix, provider count and tenure, same-store growth, de novo cohort performance.
5. **Adjusted EBITDA bridge** shown and defensible (hand off to `adjusted-ebitda-and-ev-bridge`).
6. **Risks addressed before the buyer finds them:** reimbursement exposure, provider concentration, regulatory items. Buyers re-trade on surprises.
7. **Teaser anonymity:** no name, no identifiable city combination, no unique facts that reveal the company.
8. **Charts:** labeled axes and units, sources, consistent periods, no truncated axes that exaggerate growth.
9. **Pitch book specifics:** valuation football field consistent with the analyses; buyer universe consistent with `buyer-universe-mapping`; process timeline realistic.
10. **Writing:** short headlines that state the takeaway; no unsupported superlatives; consistent tense and units.

Mark up her pages; do not rewrite them. Quote the sentence and say what is wrong and what evidence would fix it.
