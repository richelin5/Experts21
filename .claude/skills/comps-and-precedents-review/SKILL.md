# Comps and precedents review

Reference: `docs/04-valuation-methods.md` sections 1–2; market context in `docs/02-healthcare-services-ma-market.md`.

## Trading comps checklist

1. **Peer logic:** chosen by business model, payer mix, asset intensity and growth, not by SIC code. Hospitals (capex-heavy) mixed with asset-light physician services is a Major finding.
2. **Listing status:** several healthcare services names have been acquired or delisted. Check each peer is still trading.
3. **Same EV/EBITDA basis** across peers (NCI, leases, pro forma adjustments). See `adjusted-ebitda-and-ev-bridge`.
4. **Calendarization:** LTM periods end on the same date; NTM uses consensus as of the same date.
5. **Diluted shares,** current price date stated.
6. **Symmetric scrubbing:** if the target's EBITDA is adjusted, peers' EBITDA is too.
7. **Statistics:** with n = 5–8, show the median, quartiles and full range; one outlier distorts the mean.
8. **Application:** public multiples are minority and liquid. Applying them to a $5M EBITDA private company needs a size and liquidity discount and an explicit reason for it.

## Precedents checklist

1. **Vintage:** each deal's year is shown. Deals from 2021 (zero rates, roughly 7x leverage) are not a guide to 2026 pricing; weight recent deals or show them by vintage.
2. **Disclosure bias:** disclosed private-market values skew toward large or high-priced deals.
3. **EBITDA basis of the multiple:** reported, adjusted, pro forma or synergized. A "12x" on pro forma adjusted EBITDA can be 16x on reported.
4. **Platform vs. add-on:** never benchmark a platform against add-on multiples, or the reverse.
5. **Structure:** headline EV vs. cash at close; earn-outs, rollover and seller notes change the economic value.
6. **Outliers flagged:** distressed sales, strategic-synergy deals, payer mega-deals.
7. **Source:** merger proxies (DEFM14A) give the advisor's own precedent list; use them to check her set.

## Questions to ask her

- "Why is this company in the set, and what would make you drop it?"
- "If I removed your highest multiple, where does your range go?"
- "Is your precedent range consistent with what your LBO says a sponsor can pay?"

If her analysis includes a regression (multiples on growth or margin), invoke `regression-auditor` and `finance-regression-diagnostics`.
