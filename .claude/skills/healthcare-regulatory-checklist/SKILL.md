# Healthcare regulatory checklist

Reference: `docs/01-middle-market-ma-advisory.md` section 4.2; policy context in `docs/02-healthcare-services-ma-market.md`.

This is a banker's issue-spotting list, not legal advice. Findings should say "flag for counsel" where the answer depends on state law.

| Item | What to check in her work | Effect on the deal |
|---|---|---|
| **CPOM** | Does the target's state bar non-physician ownership? Is there an MSO / friendly-PC structure, and how does the management fee capture cash flow? Has the state recently tightened its rules (e.g., Oregon in 2025)? | Structure, buyer eligibility, valuation of the management fee |
| **State transaction notice/review laws** | Which states in the footprint require pre-closing notice or review (e.g., CA OHCA, OR HCMO, WA, IN, MA, NY, MN)? | 30–180+ days on the calendar; possible conditions on approval |
| **HSR / FTC** | Is the deal above the HSR threshold (adjusted annually; check FTC)? Prep time for the revised HSR form; FTC Healthcare Task Force (March 2026) and scrutiny of serial specialty roll-ups | 30-day waiting period, second-request risk, buyer-specific risk |
| **Medicare/Medicaid CHOW and enrollment** | 855A/855B filings; provider agreement assignment; payment gaps | Closing mechanics and cash flow timing |
| **Home health 36-month rule** | Majority ownership change within 36 months of initial enrollment or of the prior change | Can make an agency unsellable as structured |
| **Stark / AKS / FCA** | Referral relationships, physician ownership of ancillaries, coding audit results, prior settlements | Diligence findings, escrows and indemnities, re-trade risk |
| **Licensure and certificate of need** | State licenses, CON states for facilities | Timeline; de novo assumptions |
| **Payer consents** | Change-of-control clauses in payer contracts | Revenue at risk; closing condition |
| **Non-competes** | State enforceability for physicians | Provider retention and goodwill value |

## Questions to ask her

- "Which approvals sit on the critical path to closing, and how long are they?"
- "Which buyers on your list does this structure exclude?"
- "What would a buyer's coding audit find, and have you priced it?"
